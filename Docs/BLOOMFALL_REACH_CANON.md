`Unknown Southeast` and `unknown-southeast` were out-of-world development placeholders. They are not former in-world names, local nicknames, or lore. They may appear only in a compatibility redirect, former-placeholder metadata, immutable revision/migration evidence, tests of that compatibility, and technical topology locators whose stability preserves deterministic IDs.

This file is the single naming authority for Bloomfall Reach. The owner-approved regional package is implemented in the guarded development Codex by `apps/web/lib/bloomfall-reach-content.ts` and `apps/web/scripts/implement-bloomfall-reach-content.ts`. Production remains unchanged.

The public explanation is locked: “A cascading industrial control and containment failure during strategic load balancing drove multiple Essence storage banks into uncontrolled resonance. Isolation systems failed faster than the facility could segment the reserve, causing a catastrophic regional release.” The deeper cause remains `DEEP_MYSTERY_TRUTH = DEFERRED`.
