Date: 2026-08-25

Scope: visual-only V3/reset candidates. V1 and V2 remain preserved, inactive history.

## 1. Canon pulled back into the art direction

The reset re-read the naming authority, canonical regional architecture, implemented development Codex content, locked world topology, and local placement manifest. The rebuilt direction restores five governing truths:

1. Southreach was a state-scale reserve/refinery/gridworks and regional infrastructure district.
2. The Bloomfall contaminated air, cooling water, groundwater, drains, terrain, structures, machinery, flora and fauna.
3. The Shattercore is the active industrial source wound; the Belt is severe host-specific adaptation; the marsh is layered biological containment.
4. Blackbloom Exposure is environmental adaptation, not Seven-Phase Infusion Corruption.
5. Bellwether, Switchmother, the Last Shift and marsh coordination are defined by ecology, function and unresolved consequence—not generic monster imagery.

Full direction: `Docs/BLOOMFALL_VISUAL_RESET_V3_CANON_DIRECTION.md`.

## 4. How the reset became darker and more story-faithful

- The Shattercore now carries dead ground, reserve failure, contaminated runoff, pressure vapor, ruined evacuation systems and ongoing cycling.
- The Belt retains farms, roads, drainage, utility structures and recognizable animals while severe adaptations serve filtration, sensing, storage, armor and migration.
- The Living Marsh visibly binds and reroutes contaminated flow through water, fungi, peat, roots and animal systems.
- Human consequence appears through shelter history, evacuation remains, contaminated gear, survivors, field triage and the ambiguous Last Shift.
- Beauty comes from weather, scale, atmosphere and biological function, not sanitized surfaces.

## 5. Essence contamination representation

Air carries aerosol, vapor and charged particulate. Water carries contaminated runoff and visible filtration gradients. Soil shows salt/mineral burns and dead pockets. Steel, concrete, ceramic and reserve glass fail differently. Trees mineralize and reroute vascular growth. Reeds store and vent. Animals develop sensing, capacitance, scar tissue and altered movement. Machinery collects condensate, redirects charge and becomes biological habitat. No single effect is stamped across all hosts.

## 6. Creature and Aberrant reset

Bellwether now has exactly four load-bearing cervid limbs, continuous skull-mounted sensory antlers, conductive membrane, capacitor distortion and healed discharge damage. Its surrounding herd makes the ecological-amplifier role visible.

Switchmother is one recognizable four-limbed biological Monstrosity with clamp anchors, insulating hide, capacitor organs, routed bus/transformer connections and a sealed brood chamber assembling Latchhounds. It is neither a generic robot nor an arbitrary cable heap.

Marsh threats are embedded in hydrology, filtration and route behavior. The Last Shift remains visibly human-origin and operationally synchronized without settling personhood.
