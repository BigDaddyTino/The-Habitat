------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COMMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kingdom Management is the endgame: a live server that never pauses, a ruler
who is always a person with eyes, never a god-view. The rule of the page is
"the map is a table in your hall, your holdings run while you sleep, and the
Forge is the settlement." Every plate here is a room the ruler stands in or a
thing on the ruler's table, never a satellite view, never a strategy-game HUD.

Three plate classes:

  1. THE HERO (1 plate, 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, cinematic 16:9). The map table.
  2. THE FIVE RANKS (5 plates, 1200x800 RGB24 PNG, 3:2). One holding per rank,
     ascending from a drained riverbank plot to a throne room, so the five
     hang as a stair. Same hour of day across all five (late evening, the
     first lamps lit) so the set reads as one climb.
  3. THE SIX SIGILS (6 plates, 1024x1024 RGB24 PNG, square). Realm-tree
     emblems: a physical object, not a logo. Lit on dark. One owned color
     each. These crop to 64px on the page and must read at that size.

Style: the standing codex law — mature AAA grounded cinematic photorealism,
real lenses, real wear, near-future military-industrial kit, magic as
infrastructure. Zero text anywhere, every banner and seal BLANK. No fantasy
gloss: no gilt thrones, no velvet, no heraldic lions. Power in this world
looks like a working hall: ledgers, lock-gate levers, a Forge's light through
a door, officers who have not slept. The R rating at full weight per the
standing law where a plate calls for it (the Held Night, the Doctrine
Crisis); women striking, stated looks are locks.

Palette family for the set: the codex dark — charcoal, wet grey, bone, muted
rust, storm blue — with BRASS as the crown's owned color, used sparingly and
only on what the ruler touches: the table's edge, a seal, a lever handle, the
rim of the map. Brass earns its place; it is never wallpaper.

Story locks (hard): Commander Alder Wade is ALIVE, no statue, no fallen
statue. The First Weir's bottom is never shown. The Outfall's Anchor is
dormant. No crossroads depicted. Machines with zero anthropomorphism in their
faces. Brother Aster is never a figure — a Forge's light only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE HERO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

kingdom/hero.png — THE MAP TABLE. A ruler's hall at the first lamp of the
evening. The whole floor of the frame is a MAP built as a table: relief cast
in dark resin and brass, the river system of the Riverlands raised on it with
the five great lock-gates as real levers set into the table's edge, one of
them mid-pull under a gloved hand. Tokens on the map are not chess pieces:
they are stamped brass slugs, lead weights, a folded letter under a stone, a
cup of cold tea on a province. Around the table, cut by the frame, the court:
a Captain with a rifle slung and mud on the boots, a Chancellor with three
ledgers open, an Envoy in travelling clothes who has just arrived. The ruler
is us — the camera is the ruler's eyes at standing height, a hand in frame,
the map close. Through an open doorway at the back of the hall, the blue-
white light of a Forge chamber, steady. Rain on the high windows. No throne
in the frame; the table IS the throne. Zero text on the map; provinces are
relief, not labels.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE FIVE RANKS — one climb, five landings
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

kingdom/rank-i-freeholder.png — THE FREEHOLD. A bankside plot below Heartland
at dusk, freshly DRAINED: the cut ditches still glistening, the levee new, a
single timber-frame house half-roofed with a lamp already lit inside, a fence
line half-driven, tools left where the light ran out. Three hired hands
eating on a pallet of lumber. The river huge and calm behind. On the roof
ridge, a signal fire laid but unlit. The first kingdom: a parcel and a roof.

kingdom/rank-ii-warden.png — THE WARD. A fortified point with a job: a lock-
gate on a Riverlands leg with a squat blockhouse built onto its winch-house,
a garrison of eight in mixed kit on the wall, a patrol boat tied below, a
signal mast with a shuttered lamp. A barge queued at the lock paying its
toll at a window. The gate's chains and counterweights the size of houses.
Everything a little too small for the job it now has.

kingdom/rank-iii-magistrate.png — THE TOWNSHIP. A river town from its own
main street at evening market: districts visible as a change of roofline,
trade signs (BLANK), a courthouse porch with a magistrate's table set out
under lamps and a queue of petitioners, a militia sergeant reading an
admission list at the town gate to a family with a cart. A population that
is not yours, and the paper that makes it stay.

kingdom/rank-iv-lord.png — THE CITY. Wharves and districts under one lord:
the camera on a balcony over a working harbor at lamp-lighting, cranes and
warehouses, a grand project half-built (a stone aqueduct span, scaffolded)
cutting the skyline, a real army's columns forming on a parade ground below
by torchlight, and in the foreground on the balcony rail, a quiet argument
between two well-dressed factions of the city's own council. Factions inside
your own walls.

kingdom/rank-v-crown.png — THE KINGDOM. The hall from the hero plate, seen
from the doorway, at the moment of a Court Day: the map table crowded, six
officers seated, a vassal kneeling — not in fealty, in fatigue, a muddy
cloak — reporting, a banner of the realm hung BLANK behind the table, the
Forge light from the side door laid across the floor like a second carpet.
The ruler's chair is a plain one at the table's head, empty; we are standing
at the door, about to take it. A seat at the world's table.

3. THE SIX SIGILS — objects, not logos
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each: one object on dark, lit from one side, brass rim optional, no text, no
frame, no drop shadow. Must read at 64px.

kingdom/tree-might.png — a muster roll: a brass drum of the levy's names,
dented, with a rifle's sling looped through it. Color: rust red.
kingdom/tree-coffers.png — a toll-bar's counterweight: lead, stamped, with a
ledger's ribbon tied to it. Color: ledger white.
kingdom/tree-works.png — a lock-gate lever's brass handle, worn bright by
hands, on a slate of drainage plans. Color: brass.
kingdom/tree-arcana.png — a Forge reserve gauge: a glass vial of Essence
in a steel cradle, the level marked in days. Color: Forge blue-white.
kingdom/tree-roots.png — a granary key and an admission ledger's stamp, on
a fold of homespun. Color: moss green.
kingdom/tree-faith.png — a small shrine box, open, holding five different
tokens for five faiths and one empty slot. Color: bone.
