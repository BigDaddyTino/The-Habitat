THE COMMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These are the fifty-six pictures the codex still owes the world. Some are
founding catastrophes, some are quiet rooms where a signature changed a life,
some are things in the dark that feed. You are not decorating dossiers — you
are shooting the film stills of a world that actually happened.

This is an R-rated world and these plates may be R-rated pictures. Blood
where blood was spilled, bodies where bodies fell, dread where the dossier
carries dread. Martino's dark material runs at full weight — shoot it the
way a great cinematographer shoots a war, a haunting, a crime scene: with
total commitment and no apology. Beauty and horror are not opposites here;
half these plates are both.

AND THE LIGHT RUNS AT FULL WEIGHT TOO — owner's direction. Not everything
in Martino lives in the dark: some things see the light, and some things
ARE the light. Where a subject earns radiance — the Riverlands' hope, the
marsh keeping its word, the quietwater reliquary, a dead grid waking —
give it real, unashamed glory. And know this for every bright being this
world ever puts in front of your lens, on this run or any after: the
angelic register — the white-gold, the willing light, the beings who wear
it — is GORGEOUS, and the R rating covers the light exactly as it covers
the dark. Radiant, sensual, super sexy — the owner's own words. Desire is
as legitimate a register for the luminous as dread is for the drowned:
bare radiant skin, want in the frame, beauty that makes the viewer feel
lit up and a little weak — all of it in bounds when the subject carries
it. Bright does not mean chaste, holy does not mean plain, and the light
is never the tame half of this world.

Five plates below are marked RADIANT — the light's own slots in this run,
where you break the overcast wide open. None of the fifty-six stages an
angelic BEING; when those subjects arrive in a later run, this law is
already their brief.

Craft law, absolute as ever:
- 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, wide cinematic 16:9, center-safe for the ~564x941
  directory crop. Drop-in: apps/web/private/codex-art/<dir>/<slug>.png.
- AAA grounded cinematic photorealism. Real lenses, real weather, real wear.
  Palette: cool overcast charcoal / dirty green / wet grey / muted rust /
  storm blue / bone; one motivated warm practical per frame where earned.
  Cyan only as contained technology. White-gold only as willing light —
  and on the plates the light law claims, willing light and the sun itself
  ARE the motivated practical and may own the whole frame. Blackbloom's
  violet where the Reach demands it, always sickly, never neon. The gloom
  is the world's default, not its ceiling: when a plate below is marked
  radiant, break the overcast wide open.
- NO text ever: letters, numbers, readable pages, stencils, insignia —
  everything that would read, reads BLANK.
- THE DOSSIER IS SCRIPTURE. Read each entry before shooting it; the lines
  below are the director's notes, the dossier is the screenplay.
- Each shelf has standing plates. Yours must hang beside them like they were
  shot on the same production.

CREATURES — 2 · creatures/ · the things that moved in
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

shrieker-bat — THE CEILING THAT BREATHES. Draw Nine pump gallery, pumps dead
for years. The entire vaulted ceiling is colony: thousands of folded bodies,
a living texture you mistake for stone until the frame's one intruding
flashlight beam catches forty pairs of eyes already open. Shoot the
half-second BEFORE the panic — wings just starting to unfold at the beam's
edge, the two-second stun already inevitable. Low angle, trespasser's POV.
And remember what canon says listens for that panic from above: the frame
may keep one high corner of darkness that is not empty.

the-hollow-wing-creature — THE THING THAT HUNTS ON THE WING WITH NO SKY.
Canon, exactly: a wingspan that has no business fitting the galleries,
folded and refolded until it does; utterly silent, because the shrieker
colonies scream for it and it has learned what their panic sounds like
from above. Stage it mid-gallery in that impossible fold, half-lit — and
on the haul road beneath, its leavings as the dossier writes them: bodies
found FLAT, emptied, skin over architecture, chests opened by the feeding
structure that got very good at it. The horror plate of the run, and every
detail of it is scripture. Do not blink.

TIMELINE — 11 · timeline/ · the archive of what happened
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ten standing plates are the archive's language: documentary framing,
history-painting weight. These eleven join the record.

the-bloomfall — THE FOUNDING WOUND. Twenty years ago, the Southreach stack
let go. Shoot the minute itself: the containment structure blooming open in
slow catastrophe, energized Essence rolling across the district as a wall of
sick violet light, workers running in silhouette toward the camera and not
all of them ahead of it. The Reach's whole history in one frame. This is the
run's masterpiece slot — treat it like the plate people will point at for
years.

black-tide-at-blackweir — THE SURGE. Black contaminated water hitting the
filtration works at night: harvest crews on the platforms in storm rig,
marsh organisms breaching the tide line to meet it, spray and floodlight and
the one arm of the works already going under with a man still climbing off
it.

menders-work — THE REPAIR THAT SHOULD NOT HUM. M-17's technically successful
repair: a restored system running perfectly in a chamber where every human
artifact — dropped tools, an abandoned rating badge (BLANK), a barricade
pulled aside — says the people who knew it best chose not to fix it.

reserve-twelve — THE COUNT THAT WILL NOT CLOSE. Salvage crews stood down
outside a sealed vault level, contract gear stacked, seals unbroken, and the
evidence of the problem staged in what CAN be seen: pallet math on the
loading apron that visibly does not add up to the door it came through.

a-ledger-with-two-owners — ONE CRATE, TWO TRUTHS. Twin custody stations in
one Southreach yard, identical strategic crates on both scales, two freight
crews at hostile parade rest, and the single crate on the centerline that
both chains of custody claim. Cold bureaucratic standoff, guns not yet
part of the conversation and clearly next in it.

root-of-the-bargain — THE RETURN. Canon's exchange, exactly: crews
returning CONTAMINATED BIOMASS to a marsh sacrifice bed — laden barrows
tipped at the waterline, the dark mass sliding home — and beyond them,
already answering, a channel beginning to stand open through the reeds.
Nalia Reed's crew at the water, Ansel's instruments measuring what nobody
will call negotiation. In the reeds: not eyes, but ARRANGEMENT — the
vegetation holding a shape that attends. Coordination confirmed,
consciousness refused; the plate lives in that refusal.

the-bellwether-event — THE TURN OF THE HERDS. Migration country at dusk;
every herd on the plain faces the same wrong direction. On the horizon, the
Aberrant disturbance they face — scale suggested, not shown. Warden teams
tiny in the middle distance, deciding between redirect, observe, and kill.

the-last-safe-reading — THE HONEST GAUGE. One telemetry station still telling
the truth in a row of neighbours already lying: the operator's chair pushed
back mid-shift, dials (BLANK) in disagreement down the line, and the light
through the window the exact colour of the last normal afternoon Southreach
ever had.

the-purge-window — THE CHOICE STREET. A forecast purge, hours out: one street
carrying evacuation and containment at once — families loading a cart at one
door, sealed-suit teams bolting shutters at the next, and the sky at the
street's end already the wrong colour. The window is closing in the frame.

the-route-that-moves — THE CORRIDOR THAT WILL NOT WAIT. Orchard organisms
mid-migration have opened a passage; marsh water stands aside; a convoy
threads the gap at speed. Shoot from inside the corridor with both living
walls in motion — tomorrow this road will not exist and the picture knows.

three-failure-reports — THREE WITNESSES, ONE MACHINE. The same first-failure
staged as three witness positions around one Southreach machine hall — the
catwalk view, the floor view, the control-room glass — each sightline
genuinely supporting a different incompatible story. One frame, no split
panels: architecture does the arguing.

FLAGS — 11 · flags/ · the moments the world remembers
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flag plates are memory made physical. The six standing plates set the tone:
intimate, charged, the moment after the moment.

carries-nag — THE INHERITANCE. Tino's watch on the player's wrist — YOUR
canonical NAG design, exactly: scuffed case, twice-restitched band, torn
lug. Worn over a field jacket cuff in rain, lock-picks folded away,
eyebrow-projection dark. A dead friend's toolkit on a living arm. Continuity
with your own plate is the entire assignment.

the-first-vision — THE ROOM WITH TWO EMPTY BEDS. The player's first
episode, exactly as the dossier stages it: restraint straps hanging open,
one surgical light burning white, and against the wall two beds — BOTH
EMPTY, identical, small. No figure, no blood. The beds mean nothing to
the player yet, and the picture must hold that withheld meaning: shoot
the emptiness itself as the wrongness, a room that is a memory the
viewer has not earned. Full clinical dread; this plate is allowed to be
the most frightening image in the codex.

has-the-tino-file — THE FOLDER. Rook's desk lamp, after hours. A thin sealed
folder mid-handoff between two hands — one giving reluctantly, one taking
carefully. Contents never shown. The first true facts about Tino, and the
weight of what they cost sitting in both grips.

the-search-was-loud — SIX WEEKS BOUGHT NOTHING. A cold trail camp on a route
somebody BUILT to be run: burned-out fire, searchers' litter, a boot print
map gone to mud — and above the camp, framed so the viewer finds them
before the searchers did not: the watching positions. Empty now. They were
not empty then.

walked-the-east-road — WHAT IT COST THE WALL. Kestrel's last scouts on the
east trail of the thing that took Tino — and behind them, small and sharp
in the frame's deep field, the undermanned wall they left. Two duties in
one picture, only one of them being done.

blackweir-arm-sacrificed — THE DROWNED LIMB. One filtration arm deliberately
given to the water: submerged machinery still lit below the surface, going
dark section by section, while the surviving arms run hard above. An
amputation performed by people who loved the patient.

cairnwood-approach-lost — THE ROAD GIVEN BACK. Survey markers (BLANK)
leaning in ground reclaimed by displaced herds — and pressed into the mud
between hoofprints, the other prints, the ones that explain why the herds
moved. Nobody is coming back for the markers.

heartfen-channel-open — THE MARSH KEEPS ITS WORD · RADIANT. A channel of
open water through dense wetland that should have closed days ago: water
standing aside, reeds bowed in one direction like a held breath — and
down the channel's whole length, morning light lying on the water like a
laid table. Grace, made of landscape: something without a face is keeping
a promise, and keeping it BEAUTIFULLY. The uncanny and the luminous in
one frame, neither winning.

reserve-twelve-sealed — SETTLED IN HARDWARE. The vault door after the
ruling: containment seals welded over salvage stencils (BLANK), a
contractor's crowbar left where it was set down mid-argument. Paper lost;
steel won.

splicefield-feeder-live — THE DEAD LINE WAKES · RADIANT. An old reserve
feed carrying load again at night: one transmission run igniting across a
decommissioned sector, conductive growth flaring along the cable path like
a vein filling with light — shoot it as a RESURRECTION, jubilant and
slightly frightening at once, the single bright line writing itself across
miles of dark and every window on the route lighting one by one behind it.
The grid remembers being alive. Let the picture celebrate before it
worries.

the-last-safe-reading-recovered — OUT BEFORE THE OVERWRITE. A data core in a
courier's cradle, leaving a sector that is visibly restarting behind it —
gauge lights sweeping back to innocent zeroes down the hall. The truth got
out by minutes and the picture shows the minutes.

ITEMS — 6 · items/ · the materials the wars are about
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Still-life plates with the gravity of evidence photography. The ten standing
item plates set the bench language.

blackweir-resin — a cut block of tar-black resin on a handling cloth, faces
polished where the saw went through, and deep in the cut face the suspended
particulate that says what the marsh concentrated to make it. Handle-with-
tongs beauty.

capacitor-tissue — the disturbing one: living tissue in a lab tray, holding
charge. It should look uncomfortably like what it is — grown meat with
lightning in it — cyan strictly INSIDE the tissue, instruments keeping
their distance.

gridcore-alloy — a machined billet of strategic conductor on a scarred
bench: weapons-grade beauty, salvage provenance in every tool mark, the
kind of object men have already died over and will again.

quietwater-culture — THE RELIQUARY · RADIANT. A sealed glass vessel of
layered still water, bloom, and slow sediment — the calmest living thing
the Reach produces — lit like the sacred object it functionally is: one
shaft of clean light through the vessel, the layers glowing like a saint's
jar, the bench around it dim in deference. In a world of poisoned water,
this is the holy relic. Shoot the reverence.

reserve-glass — a graded pane with depth behind its surface that the room's
geometry cannot explain, held in a padded jig, photographed slightly off
axis because straight-on felt like being looked at.

sinkroot-fiber — coiled harvest-wet fiber, strong as cable — and the cut
end, seeping, because every metre of it was subtracted from the marsh's
own defences. The cost is in the frame.

REGIONS — 18 · regions/ · the world itself
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Environment key art. Thirty-nine standing plates set the scale; these are
the missing countries. Every one is a place a player will someday stand.

riverlands — THE FLAGSHIP · RADIANT. Martino's central watershed from
height at the moment dawn actually wins: the overcast BROKEN, sun pouring
through the tear, the braided arms running molten gold — and each arm
READABLE, the way the atlas demands, as a genuine corridor: roads and
crossings and strips of buildable green riding every branch into harder
country at the frame's edges. The world's premier building ground wearing
its best morning. Shoot it like something you could fall in love with —
players are going to live here. Pure, unguarded glory.

the-red-forest — THE CRIMSON CATHEDRAL. Inside the vast red woodland,
looking up: sun coming through a hundred metres of layered scarlet canopy
the way light comes through stained glass, columns of trunks like a nave,
the forest floor in deep red twilight — and small on that floor, one
Cartel recruitment truck with its lamp on, a pew in a church it does not
understand. Beauty first; menace arrives on the second look.

magic-torn-wasteland — THE END-GAME COUNTRY. Physical law failing in open
country: rain falling in two directions, terrain shelved at impossible
angles, a horizon that curves wrong, gravity-shed debris orbiting a low
hill. Spectacle plate — full commitment, palette law holding it together.

grand-rift — WHERE THE FOREST POURS IN. The northwest crack from the rim:
red canopy CASCADING over the edge like a slow waterfall of fire, roots
gripping the strata down the wound's layered walls, and far below the
green-black haze of depths that breathe on a rhythm nothing surface-born
keeps. Two worlds meeting at a cliff, and the forest is winning the
argument an inch a year.

high-cliffs — THE SUBLIME PLATE · RADIANT. The northern heights over Grand
Lake: waterfalls at a scale that rearranges the viewer's sense of what
water is, morning sun turning the spray into standing curtains of light,
a double rainbow rooted in the gorge, birds wheeling BENEATH the camera.
Shoot it from the cliff lip, toes-over-the-edge vertigo — awe as a
physical sensation. The lowland towns exist because of this water; make
the picture explain that without a single roof in frame.

the-desert — THE EMERALD THREAD. The southwestern hard country from
height at late sun: one tributary-fed corridor running through it like a
vein of live jade — palms, fields, standing water throwing gold — and
either side of that thread, the tan-and-bone immensity that kills the
unprepared. A compact caravan on the fertile line, indigo dots in the
green. The desert's whole politics in one contrast: everything alive is
in the thread, and everyone knows it.

long-graze — THE COUNTRY THAT REWRITES ITSELF. Canon's ecology, staged:
rootback-grazer herds carrying soil and seed across the Belt in slow
tides, blackbloom-hart strings following mineral lines the grass shows
as colour, predators written into the same field as a third readable
passage — and over the whole plain the changed posture that means the
Bellwether has entered the range: every animal's attention organised in
one direction, communicated rather than chemical, exactly as Mara Quill
insists. An ecological event space; not one building in frame.

walking-orchard — THE ACREAGE THAT LEFT. Canon: managed Southreach fruit
and windbreak acreage, joined by adaptation into vast mobile organisms
that relocate over DAYS, abandoning rising saturation — and sometimes
opening safe ground behind them. Shoot the migration mid-stride: root
masses the size of houses trailing soil and irrigation remnants, fruit
still bright in the moving crowns of what began as orchard, fiber-harvest
scaffolds abandoned against trunks that did not wait — and behind the
walkers, the clean ground opening that the-route-that-moves depends on.
Wonder and loss in the same slow stride.

blackweir — THE WORKS THAT BREATHE. The living filtration zone entire:
marsh and machinery grown into one organism across black water, harvest
platforms strung with work light — and the whole composition riding a
visible breath, water level mid-exhale along every stanchion's stain
line. Industry and ecology in an embrace neither could survive leaving.

drowned-intake — half-sunk concrete intake throats swallowed by marsh,
shallow-draft passage into the dark of them, water glass-still at the
mouths.

crown-break — the Shattercore's great rupture: the containment wound that
vents Blackbloom, violet seep against monumental broken engineering.

splicefield-substation — the grid yard as ecology at dusk: transformers
and conductive organisms grown indistinguishable, one live line humming
across the composition.

glassroot-observatory — RIGOR INSIDE THE WIRE. Meridian's field post at
dusk, built around its hardened Southreach survey tower: Dr. Ansel's lit
desk of disciplined comparison work, living samples racked and labelled
(BLANK), the famous sample case closed at the desk's edge — and along
the fence line the Reach growing over the wire, instrumented rather than
fought, monitored growth wearing sensor collars. A station whose whole
discipline is measuring coordination while refusing to call it
consciousness; the picture holds that refusal in its tidiness.

reedless-mile — the open reach where growth refuses: bare water and mud in
a fertile world, a marked route (BLANK posts) visibly not going where it
went yesterday.

ashline-exchange — THE JUNCTION THAT FAILED AS A HUMANE SYSTEM. Canon's
words. Converging rail and road where trains, evacuees, and cordons met
for four days after the Bloomfall: barrier rails still bowed where the
crowd pressed, buried freight cars under the aprons that salvagers work
for gridcore, personal effects still surfacing in the spoil. A working
controlled junction today — the traffic just does not look down. No
fire, no bodies: the bent steel remembers for everyone.

redline-shelter-six — the sealed shelter: worn door markings (BLANK), the
approach litter of the people who reached it and the belongings of those
who did not, early Last Shift evidence in the seal's wrongness. Casualty
record as landscape; shoot it like a war grave, R-rated in what it
implies and archaeological in what it shows.

reserve-vault-twelve — the strategic level itself: stacks in darkness
receding past the lamp's reach, and staged in the count of visible crates
the wrongness the ledgers cannot reconcile.

stormglass-recruitment-camp — the Cartel intake under Red Forest canopy:
Wendy's table centered, wardrobe pod right, queue posts strung with rain
tarps. YOUR OWN SEQ-000 scene — contradict nothing you authored. Anonymous
queue figures allowed, faces averted; the table lamp is the frame's one
warmth and it is not warm.

SYSTEMS — 8 · systems/ · the Reach's rules made visible
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
System plates teach a rule in one image. All eight are Bloomfall Reach —
keep one visual signature across them, check the bloomfall review packages
for owner-approved candidates first; approval outranks regeneration.

adaptive-mutation — THE SIGNATURE RULE. One creature, one frame, no split
panels: the wound it escaped with still visible under what the wound BECAME
— scar tissue transitioned into armament. Wound it and let it go, and it
heals into something worse; make one animal teach that entire sentence.

aberrant-escalation — one silhouette on a ridge changing the posture of an
entire valley below: herds turned, camps striking, Wardens converging —
and pinned under a stone in the foreground, a hand-drawn map (BLANK) that
shows what the party KNOWS, which is visibly less than the valley does.

blackbloom-exposure — contamination as biography: a surveyor's forearm
beside a stained boundary line, dosim badge (BLANK) gone dark, the ground's
violet capillaries and the skin's faint answering ones in the same light.
Distinct from corruption, says the dossier — make the difference legible.

bloomfall-environmental-hazards — the hazard sheet as one traverse: a
crossing party mid-route through saturation shimmer, ground that has
already taken one of their marker poles, and the storm light arriving that
reclassifies everything they just crossed.

bloomfall-travel — four routes in one country, one frame: the maintained
road with its checkpoint, the known detour worn by feet, the negotiated
water with its offering post, and the fourth way — unmarked ground nobody
signs for — exactly as inviting and wrong as it should be.

bloomstorms — stage three of five on the horizon: contaminated weather you
can READ, structured like a creature's anatomy in cloud, the two stages of
warning already legible in the light and the fleeing traffic on the road
beneath.

essence-saturation — one valley wearing all four bands as a gradient:
Residual's near-normal green, Active's shimmer, Surge's standing
distortion, and at the valley's head the Bloomstorm band where the air
itself has an opinion. A legend without words.

marsh-absorption — the marsh at its work, landscape scale: binding lines
of root drawing contamination down, a sacrificed grove going dark so the
water beyond it can stay bright — the same intelligence the bargain plates
negotiate with, seen doing what it does when nobody trades with it.
