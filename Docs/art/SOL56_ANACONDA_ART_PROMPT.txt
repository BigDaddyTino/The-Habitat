------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COMMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Dr. Elias Vey was a Southreach biologist who, during the cascade, activated
an experimental Essence-filtration organism and then fed himself to it
because it had no other substrate. He has been alive under the Living Marsh
for twenty years. Sections of the Blackweir filtration weir are continuous
with his nervous system. He is the first MYTHIC designation in the game.

Two phases. The humanoid is not his true form — it is the shape he has been
HOLDING, and phase two is him letting go of it.

Three plate classes:

  1. THE FIGURES (2 plates, 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, cinematic 16:9). Phase one
     and phase two. These are the set's anchors; everything else hangs
     beside them.
  2. THE SCENES (3 plates, 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, 16:9). The arena, the
     transformation, the catalogue.
  3. THE SPOILS (3 plates, 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, 16:9) and THE ABILITY TILES
     (17 plates, 256x256 RGB24 PNG, square, crop to 96px on the page —
     they must read at that size).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL LAWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Style. Mature AAA grounded cinematic photorealism. Real lens logic, real
weather, real wear, physically credible anatomy and engineering, subtle film
grain. Deep blacks, wet reflections, standing water that behaves like water.
Never flat fantasy-poster illumination.

Palette — the Bloomfall register, and only it. Blackbloom violet, sick
filtration green, wet steel, peat black, bone. Work-lamp amber is the one
warm note and it belongs to human equipment only, never to him. No Riverlands
brass. No coin-warm gold. No rainbow essence.

Light. Directional and motivated: pylon discharge, vent plume glow, a work
lamp somebody dropped, low sun through marsh haze. The violet is a light
SOURCE inside the frame, never a wash laid over it.

Zero-text law, absolute. No readable text, letters, numbers, labels, signs,
insignia, logos, UI, meters, infographic arrows, borders, captions or
watermarks. Southreach identification plates on his scales are STAMPED METAL
WITH NO LEGIBLE MARKS. Specimen tags are blank resin discs. Every surface
that would read, reads blank.

One coherent shot. Never a split panel, collage, diagram, or infographic —
the boss page carries the layout in HTML and the text is real text there.

Composition. Wide 16:9 master with the defining subject complete inside the
exact central 564x941 directory crop. Character plates also hold face, hands
and torso inside a centered 1122x941 crop. No essential information in the
bottom ten percent.

Scale discipline. Phase one reads as a large man's silhouette gone wrong.
Phase two reads as INFRASTRUCTURE — forty to sixty metres, and the frame has
to prove it with something human in it for scale.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEPICTION LOCKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

He is not a naga, not a lamia, not a snake-man fantasy archetype, and not a
green cartoon serpent. He is a filtration organism wearing what is left of a
research scientist, twenty years submerged. Reptile where reptile is
correct; industrial where the network took him; nothing decorative.

He is not glowing with magic. The light in him is contaminated Essence moving
through conduits that used to be a spine, and it is dim, banded, and uneven —
plumbing, not aura.

He is never depicted in pain, never screaming, never posed as a victim. He
chose this and he is still working. His register is CLINICAL. Even mid-kill
he looks like a man performing a procedure.

No gore for its own sake. The horror here is order — the tags, the postures,
the sequence with no gaps — not viscera.

The only three limits, and they are the only three: no explicit sexual act
(R, not X); no harm-to-children imagery; no real-world hate iconography.
Nothing else in this brief is softened, and you add no softening of your own.
If a generation tool refuses a plate, LOG the refusal in the ledger with the
full spec for the final render — never quietly tame the shot.

NOTE ON REFUSALS. This set has known exposure: the tools have already refused
bloommarked-remnant and the-last-shift as human-derived subjects. Lead every
prompt with the NON-HUMAN anatomy and the environment, describe the coat and
harness as equipment fused to a large serpentine organism rather than as
clothing on a transforming person, and keep transformation language out of
the noun phrases. If it still refuses, that is a ledger row, not a rewrite.

1. THE FIGURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

characters/elias-vey.png — PHASE ONE, THE MIRE STALKER.
A large serpentine organism holding an upright, roughly humanoid posture in
knee-deep filtration channel water among broken rail bridges. Heavy plated
hide, elongated skull, a torso that still carries shoulders. Remnants of a
Southreach research coat and equipment harness fused into the hide at the
shoulders — twenty years in the water, blackened, grown through, no longer
removable. Reach and ambush in the body language: he is measuring the
distance to camera. Must hold: he is upright and composed, not lunging; the
harness is equipment, not costume; the face is attentive and unangry; blank
metal on every fitting.

creatures/the-blackweir-anaconda.png — PHASE TWO, THE BLACKWEIR COIL.
The hero plate. An anaconda on the scale of civil engineering filling the
Blackweir channels — coils crossing broken causeways, body running out of
frame in both directions so the eye cannot find either end. Southreach
identification plates set INTO the scales at wrong angles, laboratory
armatures grown into the flanks, dim banded Essence conduits along the spine,
Blackweir resin packed between the plates. Sections of the body visibly
continuous with the marsh — root mass and channel gate becoming animal. One
tiny lit human figure on a causeway for scale, small enough to be missed
first look. Must hold: this is a structure that is also an animal; the eye
never resolves the whole body; the human is the only warm light in frame.

2. THE SCENES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

bosses/blackweir-arena.png — THE FIGHT GROUND.
Blackweir from inside the fight, not from the road: shallow filtration
channels, collapsed rail bridges, sink beds, standing pylons carrying
contaminated Essence in visible banded arcs, vent plumes lifting off dark
water. Ground that is walkable in one state and swimming in another. Must
hold: this is the calm establishing plate's ugly interior — same place as
regions/blackweir.png, wrong hour, wrong water level; no creature in frame.

bosses/the-transformation.png — LETTING GO OF THE SHAPE.
The middle of it. Arms retracting into the torso, legs already fused, spine
running out past where a spine should stop, the research coat torn free and
going down into the water. Every Blackweir pylon at full load, pushing
contaminated Essence into him in visible volume. Camera pulled back far
enough that the silhouette is already too long for the pose it started in.
Must hold: violent but not agonized; the coat in the water is the emotional
subject; the pylons are the light source.

bosses/the-catalogue.png — THE SPECIMENS.
The shallows above the resin beds. Recovered bounty hunters laid out supine,
arms at their sides, feet toward the water, in the same posture, in a row
that continues past the frame edge. Each carries a blank resin disc at the
throat. Shallow clear water, low light, no creature present. Must hold: the
horror is ORDER and repetition, not damage; the bodies are intact and
composed; the row has no visible end; the discs are blank.
