------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART ONE — THE BACKDROPS (8)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT THEY ARE
The talent calculator (/codex/talents) draws each class's tree as icon tiles
in six columns, and when a node is inspected it draws the road to it as
constellation lines — brass dashes, cyan-white junction stars. Those lines
are the existing constellation grammar and they STAY. This run puts a scene
behind them. The page lays a dark wash (78–94% black-green) over the image
so the tiles read; design for that wash, not against it.

FORMAT
- Landscape 1672x941 PNG, house convention, RGB24, no alpha.
- The board is wide and can scroll; the image is cover-fit and centred.
  Keep the subject in the middle 60% horizontally and clear of the top
  80px (the branch headers sit there).
- One image per class. No text, no glyphs, no insignia.

THE STYLE — A PLACE, NOT A PERSON
The class plates (SOL56_CLASS_ART) already gave each class a body. The
backdrop is the GROUND that class stands on: an environment at night or
dusk, near-future military-industrial, the codex dark palette (charcoal,
wet grey, bone, muted rust, storm blue) with the class's ONE owned colour
as the single light source. Almost no figures; if a silhouette appears it
is small, unlit, and unreadable as a face. Depth of field deep, lens wide,
horizon low. Under the wash the image should read as texture and one
motivated glow, the way a star chart reads over a dark sky.

THE EIGHT GROUNDS
1. bastion — THE GATE: a breached gatehouse at night, sandbagged doorway,
   a Containment ward across it as a faint plane of light; brass glow
   from a burning barrel behind the line. Plates and shotgun shells on the
   ground; nobody standing in it.
2. spector — THE LONG EYE: a rooftop at 3am over a wet city; a rifle's
   bipod at the parapet edge, scope caps open, a single distant window
   lit cold grey-blue across the gap. The whole city is the sight picture.
3. conduit — THE LANTERN: a siege trench at dusk, a caster's ward-line
   glowing lantern-gold along the parapet, pale light venting from a rig
   left on a crate; artillery haze on the horizon, no shells in flight.
4. surger — THE PULSE: a dosing bench in a corrugated shelter, red rig
   telltales, a heartbeat-trace line scratched into the wall, harvested
   material in jars; everything wet, everything red-lit from one source.
5. archon — THE CHORUS: a high roost at dusk — perches, a saddle rack, a
   drone charging cradle, a covered shape that could be a raised body;
   green-gold canopy light through a broken roof; thermals rising.
6. procurator — THE LEDGER: a map table in a command tent, ledger-white
   lamp on the sand table, tokens on a grid, a horn hanging from the pole,
   the tent flap open on a column of lights on a road below.
7. cypherist — THE CIRCUIT: a field bench under a tarp — an exoframe
   half-open on its stand, capacitor cells racked and glowing contained
   cyan, a turret's tripod, cables into a cracked grid junction.
8. maverick — THE CROSSED IRONS: a dusty street at the hour a duel gets
   called — one lit doorway, ember-orange, two lines of boot prints in the
   dust meeting at ten paces; a holster on a post, a stormglass round on
   the ground catching the light.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART TWO — THE ICONS (394 talent · 108 spell) AND PLATES (20 skill)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT THEY ARE
Small square pictures the way a WoW or FF14 action icon works: one object,
one gesture, or one effect, legible at 34px on the tree and at 40px on a
card. The page shows a two-letter glyph until each file lands, so deliver
in batches by class; nothing breaks while the set is partial.

FORMAT
- 256x256 PNG, RGB24, square, no alpha, no border (the page draws the
  frame). Centre the subject; leave a 10% margin.
- Filenames EXACTLY as listed by the generator below. A misnamed file is
  simply invisible.

THE STYLE — ONE SET, FOUR FAMILIES
- Ground: the codex dark (near-black charcoal-green vignette). Subject lit
  by one source. Painterly-real, not flat vector: these sit beside
  photoreal plates and must not read as clip art.
- TALENT icons use the class's owned colour as the light (Bastion brass,
  Spector grey-blue, Conduit gold, Surger red, Archon green-gold,
  Procurator ledger-white, Cypherist cyan, Maverick ember). Passive nodes:
  an object at rest. Active nodes: a gesture or impact, motion blur
  allowed. Spell-unlock nodes: the effect itself, with Essence cyan-white
  in it. Choice nodes: the object split or doubled. Capstones: the same
  grammar with a thin brass ring inside the margin — the ONLY decoration
  allowed in the set. Corrupted branch (7 per class, ids corrupt-1..7):
  the class's object with violet veining; the seventh is the object gone.
- SPELL icons use the pillar's colour: Thermodynamics warm white-gold,
  Kinetics steel, Structure slate with a cyan seam, Biologics wet red-
  brown, Cognition pale violet-grey, Resonance deep cyan. Licensed: the
  effect small. Certified: the effect large. Master: the effect and its
  cost in one frame (Sublimation is steam inside a sealed helmet).
- SKILL plates are 512x512, same style, one tool or one hand doing the
  skill's thing (Marksmanship: a thumb on a scope turret; Negotiation: two
  cups, one moved). The skill page shows them at 96px.

HARD PROHIBITIONS
- No text, numerals, letters, runes or fake script. No faces. No logos.
- No fantasy gloss: no glowing swords, no spellbooks, no wizard hands.
  Magic is infrastructure — rigs, wards, cells, doses, veining.
- No two icons in a class that differ only by colour. If two nodes are
  the same idea at different strengths (Adjust / Battle Form), change the
  object, not the tint.

THE LIST
Run, from apps/web:

  npx tsx scripts/list-art-slots.ts

It prints every required filename with the node's name, its type, its
flavor line and its effect line — the brief per icon is those four lines.
Read the effect line before drawing; it is what the icon must mean.

SELF-QA BEFORE YOU REPORT DONE
- View every icon at 34px. If it does not read, redraw it.
- All eight classes' icons side by side: one set, one light grammar.
- Backdrops viewed WITH the page wash (open /codex/talents): tiles legible,
  the road lines visible over the picture, the picture still a place.
- Zero text anywhere.
