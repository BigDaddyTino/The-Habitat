------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COMMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The catch-up run shot the world's missing memories. THIS run shoots the
world's beating heart: the one country where dawn still wins, the machinery
of crowns, the faiths people actually kneel to, the soulless things that
stand on walls, and the people holding a lit fuse with perfect manners.
You are not decorating dossiers — you are shooting the film stills of a
world that actually happened, and this is its most alive chapter.

This is an R-rated world and these plates are R-rated pictures, in BOTH of
its registers, at full weight, no apology:

THE DARK AT FULL WEIGHT. Where a subject carries dread, shoot the dread —
the Collector's unhurried tread, the stopped cars at Deadhaul, the fen past
the last levee, the tannin water where the dead trade keeps its books.
Blood where blood was spilled, menace where the dossier carries menace.
Shoot it the way a great cinematographer shoots a war, a haunting, a debt
coming due: total commitment.

AND THE LIGHT AT FULL WEIGHT — this run is the light's FLAGSHIP campaign.
The Riverlands are the RADIANT half of the world made ground: white-gold
dawn over braided water, the country worth fighting FOR. Where a subject
earns radiance, break the overcast wide open and give it real, unashamed
glory. And the standing law holds for every luminous being and bright
subject this world ever puts in front of your lens: the angelic register is
GORGEOUS — radiant, sensual, super sexy, the owner's own words. Desire is
as legitimate a register for the luminous as dread is for the drowned.
Bright does not mean chaste, holy does not mean plain, and the light is
never the tame half of this world. The women of this run — the Judge, the
Factor, the Widow, the Captain — are drawn attractive, striking, at full
presence; stated looks in their blocks are locks.

The ONLY limits, and they are the only three: no explicit sexual acts
(R, not X); no harm-to-children imagery; no real-world hate iconography.
Nothing else in this brief is softened, and you add no softening of your
own. If a generation tool refuses a plate, LOG the refusal in the ledger
and write the full spec for the final render — never quietly tame the shot.

Craft law, absolute as ever:
- REGION / SYSTEM / THREAD plates: 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, wide cinematic 16:9,
  key subject center-safe for the ~564x941 directory crop.
  CHARACTER portraits: match the delivered trainer-portrait set exactly.
  CREATURE plates: match the standing creature-dossier set.
  Drop-in: apps/web/private/codex-art/<dir>/<slug>.png — the brief names
  every path.
- AAA grounded cinematic photorealism. Real lenses, real weather, real wear.
  THE PER-LEG PALETTES in the brief are law — five river legs, five color
  identities, so eighty water plates never blur into one river. White-gold
  dawn and living river green own the radiant plates at full luminous
  weight; storm blue, wet grey, charcoal, bone, and rust hold the edges.
  NO Blackbloom violet anywhere in the Riverlands. Cyan only as contained
  technology on Stormgate hardware.
- NO text ever: letters, numbers, readable pages, ledgers, stencils,
  insignia, price lists — everything that would read, reads BLANK.
- THE DOSSIER IS SCRIPTURE. Every plate's entry is live in the codex — read
  it before shooting. The brief's blocks are the director's notes; the
  dossier is the screenplay; where they disagree, the dossier wins and the
  discrepancy goes in the ledger.
- Each shelf has standing plates. Yours must hang beside them like they
  were shot on the same production.

Story locks that override everything (the brief carries the full list):
- Commander Alder Wade is ALIVE. No fallen statue, no funeral, no
  assassination imagery anywhere in the run. The statue appears only
  shrouded, uncrated, or under construction.
- The First Weir's true bottom is never shown; its darkness continues past
  every light source.
- The Outfall's Anchor is DORMANT — no activation, no Breach, no glow the
  fen didn't bring.
- Deadhaul's stopped cars are never shown open or unloaded; no
  falls-swifts in that sky.
- The Machines have NO faces that emote — zero anthropomorphism; menace
  from purpose, not expression. The Collector is never hurried.
- Brother Aster is already delivered and is never a figure. The crossroads
  bargain is never depicted. Species locks on every portrait are locks.

Shooting order doctrine:
1. The five LEG plates first (arcadia-gate, cliffgate, riftgate, sandgate,
   stormgate) — they set each leg's palette; then heartland, the flagship
   city plate of the whole world.
2. Each leg's children against their leg plate for continuity — forts,
   towns, villages, outposts, charters.
3. first-weir and the-outfall — the two doors, one drowned, one dormant.
4. the-waterworks system plate, then the six FAITH plates (systems/) —
   diegetic devotion, no floating deities; banking is the horror on the
   Communion plate.
5. The MACHINES (creatures/) — the shelf plate then the seven patterns.
6. The PEOPLE (characters/) — eight portraits; the trainer-set conventions,
   the standing depiction laws, and every stated look a lock.
7. The remaining CREATURES (creatures/) — the seven riverlands species.
8. threads/the-fuse-at-heartland last — the shrouded statue at dawn, the
   city pretending not to watch itself. Nothing has happened yet.
   Everything is about to. Close the run on it.
