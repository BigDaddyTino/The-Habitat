------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE COMMISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The talents page already has each class as a star chart. This run gives each
one a BODY: one figure, one moment, the class's whole argument in a single
frame the way a fighting game's character select sells a fighter. These are
the eight faces of the game's build system; a player should be able to pick
their class from these plates without reading a word.

Format: 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, cinematic 16:9. Two crops must both read: the
dossier hero (right-hand panel, roughly the center 45% of the width at full
height) and the shelf card (a 3:4 portrait crop centered at 30% height). Put
the figure's head and hands inside that portrait window; let the world spill
into the side fields.

Style: the standing character-portrait law — mature AAA grounded cinematic
photorealism, real lenses, real wear, species and looks as locks — but with
MOTION: these are action stills, not portraits. The R rating at full weight
in both registers, per the standing law; women striking, stated looks locks.
Zero text anywhere; all insignia blank. No fantasy-gloss armor: near-future
military-industrial kit, magic as infrastructure.

Craft law for the set: one palette family across all eight (the codex dark —
charcoal, wet grey, bone, muted rust, storm blue) so they hang as a set, each
with ONE motivated color the class owns: Bastion brass, Spector cold
grey-blue, Conduit lantern-gold, Surger red, Archon green-gold, Procurator
ledger-white, Cypherist contained cyan, Maverick ember.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE EIGHT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

classes/bastion.png — THE DOORWAY. A plated soldier filling a breached
doorway from the inside, shotgun low, a Seal ward standing in the frame like
heat-shimmer with weight. Behind, the squad moving inside the ward. The wall
that answers back.

classes/spector.png — ONE ROUND. A marksman prone on a wet rooftop at night,
eye still to the glass a half-second after the shot, the city not yet aware.
The suppressed carbine beside them like a second opinion. Nobody was there.

classes/conduit.png — THE LANTERN. A caster mid-cast in a siege line, pool
light pouring from the hands, artillery-precise: a grid square burning
behind them and not one ally touched. A sidearm holstered and forgotten.

classes/surger.png — THE ENGINE. An infused berserker mid-swing, rig on the
back running hot, veins lit under the skin at the phase-two edge, blade
wet. Damage as fuel, visibly. The one plate allowed to look like it enjoys
this.

classes/archon.png — THE CHORUS. A beastmaster on a war mount at altitude —
the Unridden's sheet silhouette exactly for a hypogriff if the mount is one
— with a flock of bonded shapes rising around them. More of you than the
fight started with.

classes/procurator.png — THE SAND TABLE. A commander at a field map table
under a tarp, hand on the radial order wheel's physical analogue, three
squads' worth of people looking at them. Weak alone, unbeatable in numbers
— shoot the numbers.

classes/cypherist.png — BENCH ANYWHERE. A tech in a half-open warframe at a
field bench, drones lifting off the table, cyan contained to the tool
lights. The remote war: one eye on the frame, one on the feed.

classes/maverick.png — TWIN IRONS. A gunslinger at the moment of the draw
in a Southside street, two pistols or pistol and ember-hand (Iron & Ember —
show the spell-hand lit), coat moving, the crowd already stepped back. Loud,
fast, seen.

Gate 6b stands: bright plates at full weight where the class earns it —
the Conduit's lantern and the Maverick's ember own their frames.
