Style: the standing codex law — mature AAA grounded cinematic photorealism,
real lenses, real wear, near-future military-industrial kit, magic as
infrastructure; zero text, all insignia blank; women striking, stated looks
are locks. Story locks: Brother Aster is NEVER a figure (Core light only);
the Forge is a pedestal in rings and cabling, never a glowing altar; no
crosses, no halos, no fantasy-church iconography anywhere. A faith in this
world looks like a working hall with a ledger.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
factions/the-congregation-of-the-bound.png — THE FIRST HOUR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1672x941 16:9. A Forge hall at night, industrial: the pedestal in its
containment rings, the Core's blue-white light steady (never flaring), the
cabling bolted to old stone. On the platform, a reclaimed body sitting up in
the first hour — a soldier in a fresh-issue undershirt, disoriented, alive.
Beside them on a plain stool, a Sexton: dark plain coat, a ledger-clip at the
belt, one hand flat on the platform's edge, not on the person, saying
nothing. Behind, the queue: a dozen people on benches under one work lamp,
waiting their turn with the patience of a parish. On the wall, a ledger board
of tally marks with NO legible text. Menace absent; this is the gentlest
plate in the codex and the most unsettling for it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1024x1024, flat emblem on transparent or dark. A containment ring (the Forge's
rings, seen edge-on as a circle) with a single tally-stroke through it, and a
small bell hung from the ring's lower edge. Steel and bone-white, one line of
Core blue. No text. Must read at 32px.

characters/the-sexton-of-heartland.png — SEXTON EDDA BROOK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Portrait law (1672x941 or 3:4 per the character-bible brief). Fifties, broad,
grey-cropped, a river-worker's forearms under the Sexton's plain dark coat,
the ledger-clip at her belt worn like a key. She sits on the stool beside the
Heartland platform; the Resident's Core light (NO figure, ever — a steady
blue-white from the rings behind her) lays across one side of her face. In
her lap, a closed folder she is not opening. Expression: dry, exact, the
patience of someone who counts deaths for a living and is not tired of the
living. Locks: she is not soft, not saintly, not lit like a saint.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
characters/the-wellkeeper-of-honest-well.png — ANOUK SARR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Portrait law. Sixties, lean, sun-dark, indigo keeper-cloth like the Standing
Camp clans', a coil of well-rope over one shoulder, mending in her hands,
eyes down. Behind her at noon: the Honest Well's stone lip and, out of focus,
two feuding clans' riders drinking from the same bucket in silence, weapons
slung. The desert white with heat, the indigo the only saturated color.
Locks: she is a host, not a priest — nothing ceremonial on her; the rope is
the only ornament and it is not for water.
