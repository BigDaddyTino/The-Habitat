STANDING DIRECTIONS (owner's, load-bearing, same as your prior runs)
==============================================================================

- Format: 1672x941 RGB24 PNG, wide cinematic 16:9, center-safe for the
  directory-card crop (~564x941 center window) and the responsive crop.
- Style: mature AAA grounded cinematic photorealism; hard-lived production
  design; cool overcast charcoal / dirty green / wet grey / muted rust / storm
  blue / bone, one restrained motivated warm practical. Cyan only as contained
  technology; white-gold only as contained willing light.
- NO text, letters, numbers, labels, readable papers, logos, insignia, UI,
  watermarks, borders, split panels.
- Species and state locks are LOAD-BEARING. Every subject block below names
  what the keeper IS. Do not soften a nonhuman into a costume, and do not
  drift a stated look toward a default.
- THE ROSTER RULING (owner, this run): the bench is not all humanoid, and the
  humanoids are NOT all white. Each human subject's block states their look —
  skin, features, region — and that statement is a lock, not a suggestion.
- Any female portraits have appealing features — we like our women to look
  good. This coexists with the species and ethnicity locks; it never overrides
  them.
- References are style-only. Never copy an identity.

medicine.png — THE HEALER'S TABLE. A field surgery mid-pause: instrument roll
open, one basin, sutures, a phase-reading instrument with its dial turned away
from camera (unreadable), a cot's edge. The patient just left alive. One warm
practical over the table.

refining.png — THE GRADE. A refinery bench: containment glass racked by size,
one crate open with graded doses seated in straw, a brass scale, and one sealed
crate apart from the others — the one whose provenance somebody read and did
not say aloud. No glow beyond a contained cool shimmer in the glass.

chemistry.png — THE BENCH. A working chemistry bench, near-future not fantasy:
burners, condensers, a rack of field vials, one stormglass shard clamped for
stabilising, faint fume. Everything labelled with BLANK tags (no text law).

engineering.png — THE HALL PIECE. A machinist's bench holding one beautiful
finished piece — a sealed infusion rig — surrounded by the honest mess that
made it: swarf, calipers, a prosthetic hand clamped mid-fit, tool wall behind.

logistics.png — THE LEDGER AND THE HORN. A quartermaster's table: a thick
closed ledger, dose tins counted into ranks, ammunition boxed and chalk-marked
with BLANK marks, and hanging above the table a battered signal horn. Everything
squared to the grain of the wood.

architecture.png — THE WALL. A fortification section under construction at
dusk: the ninth course going in, load-path bracing exposed, a collapse-plan
sand table in the foreground with BLANK markers. Scale figures absent; their
scaffolds present.

extraction.png — THE PARTIAL TAKE. A claim face where the vein is half-worked
and deliberately stopped: clean tool marks ending at a chalk line (BLANK), the
living seam left intact, a rack of samples, one lantern. The restraint IS the
image.

culinary.png — THE REAL MEAL. A long camp table just before the company sits:
steam off real food, mismatched tin plates set with care, one good knife, bread
broken not cut, rain on the canvas above. The only warm image in the nine —
earn it.

xenobiology.png — THE HANDLER'S YARD. A husbandry yard at first light: an open
travel crate, harness and plate-cutting tools on a rail, feed buckets, and one
large animal presence implied — shadow, dented rail, wet prints — without the
animal in frame.
