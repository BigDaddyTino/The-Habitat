SOL 5.6 — CONSTELLATION RUN: THE EIGHT TREES' CLASS CONSTELLATIONS
===================================================================

Eight single still images, one per character class, for the talent
calculator at /codex/talents. This is NOT the cinematic-realism brief you
ran for the codex dossiers — these are STAR CHARTS. Read the difference
before you generate anything.

FORMAT
------
- Landscape 1672x941 PNG, house convention. The calculator crops to a
  wide banner (center-weighted, ~300px tall on screen) — keep the whole
  constellation inside the middle two-thirds vertically.
- One image per class. No variants, no contact sheets.

THE STYLE — A STAR CHART, NOT A SCENE
-------------------------------------
- Ground: near-black charcoal-green, matching the codex UI's dark panels.
  A breath of nebula haze is allowed if it stays dim.
- The class image is drawn AS A CONSTELLATION: points of light joined by
  hairline strokes, the way a star atlas draws a hunter from eleven stars.
  The viewer should see the shape the way they see Orion — suggested,
  not illustrated. NO drawn figures, NO rendered objects, NO scenery.
- Stars: Essence cyan-white, varying sizes (capstones read as the
  brightest stars). Linework: faint brass, one hairline weight.
- Each chart carries ONE dim VIOLET cluster of seven small stars along
  its lower edge, slightly apart from the main figure — the corrupted
  branch. Same position language on all eight, the seventh star faintest.
- The eight are a SET: same ground, same star rendering, same line
  weight, same violet cluster convention. Design the grammar once on
  Bastion and hold it.

HARD PROHIBITIONS
-----------------
- NO text, numbers, labels, glyphs, or fake script anywhere.
- NO figures, faces, creatures, or objects rendered as art — only star
  points and connecting lines forming the suggestion.
- No lens flares, no bright nebula soup, no astrology symbols.

THE EIGHT CONSTELLATIONS — what each figure suggests
----------------------------------------------------
1. bastion — THE GATE: a gatehouse arch. The core spine is the keystone
   course; two towers (Shieldwall, Breacher); a ward-light star above the
   arch (Aegis); heavy foundation stars (Juggernaut, Last Stand).
2. spector — THE LONG EYE: a rifle in profile, stock to muzzle. Branches
   hang like scope rings and rail fittings. Two nearly-twin stars at the
   muzzle — one bright (the loud round), one barely there (the quiet one).
3. conduit — THE LANTERN: a six-rayed lantern; the core is the flame, each
   branch a ray. Two stars inside the flame itself sit close but never
   touch — the deep reservoir and the live wire.
4. surger — THE PULSE: a heartbeat trace crossing a faint body silhouette
   (suggested by outline stars only). Branches hang off each spike. The
   biggest spike carries the fork pair.
5. archon — THE CHORUS: a raised hand ringed by orbiting shapes — a beast
   form, a machine form, a summoned form, a still form — each orbit one
   branch, all drawn from the same star grammar.
6. procurator — THE LEDGER: balance scales over a faint map grid. The core
   spine is the beam; branches hang from both pans; two stars at the
   fulcrum — the one who stands on the field, the one who stands above it.
7. cypherist — THE CIRCUIT: a closed circuit diagram; the core is the
   power rail, each branch a parallel circuit. FOUR switch stars (this is
   the only chart with two fork pairs) drawn as breaks in the line that
   only bridge one way.
8. maverick — THE CROSSED IRONS: two pistols crossed at the cylinder. The
   core climbs the X where they meet; branches fan out like ejected
   casings; the two muzzle stars are the fork — both iron, or iron and
   ember (make the ember muzzle's star faintly warm, the one warm note
   allowed in the set).
