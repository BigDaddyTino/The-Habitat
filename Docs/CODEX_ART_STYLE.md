# Martino Story Codex art direction

Codex art is mature AAA key art for a rugged magical-industrial world: cinematic realism, practical grime, human stakes, and ancient power forced through machinery that was never built to understand it.

## Visual language

- **Palette:** charcoal, wet steel, storm blue, oxidized brass, jungle green, bone, and sun-bleached stone. Saturated color is an authored signal: electric blue/cyan for contained technology, violet for unstable corruption or rift exposure, ember red for hostile supernatural power, and living gold/white for rare willing magic.
- **Lighting:** directional and motivated. Use storm breaks, low sun, work lamps, furnace glow, ward light, or cold laboratory spill. Preserve deep blacks and wet reflections; avoid flat fantasy-poster illumination.
- **Materials:** everything has weight and history—scarred steel, riveted brass, oiled leather, salt-stained concrete, imported marble, wet timber, dense foliage, glass vessels, cables, field repairs, and lived-in cloth. Technology is rugged infrastructure, not clean sci-fi chrome.
- **Framing:** landscape key art around 16:9, composed for a center crop on library cards. Characters read at three-quarter or environmental portrait scale; creatures show clear anatomy and habitat; factions show people doing the work of the faction; regions establish geography and social function before spectacle.
- **Essence:** a scarce substance held in vessels, conduits, veins, or disciplined spell structures. Its light affects nearby skin and materials. It never reads as decorative glitter.
- **Corruption:** invasive and anatomical—subdermal branching, asymmetry, pressure beneath skin or armor, wrong growth, and environmental contamination. Phase matters. Never escalate a named character or creature beyond the phase established in canon.
- **Tone:** 17+ through consequence, danger, bodily cost, and credible violence; never through pin-up framing, gore for its own sake, or weightless power fantasy.

## Canon boundaries

Do not resolve protected mysteries in art: what is under the war, Tino's corruption phase, Amanda's species, spoiler-gated actual status, the cause of the Drain, or any silhouette/insignia/biology that would identify an answer the prose withholds. Show aftermath, pressure, partial silhouettes, or human reaction instead. If the dossier cannot support a specific visual without invention, leave the placeholder and return it as a writing gap.

No text, lettering, UI, watermark, modern brand, or legible invented insignia belongs inside generated key art. Faction logos remain separate transparent assets.
