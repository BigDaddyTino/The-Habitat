## Canon recovered and its visual consequence

### Southreach before the disaster

Southreach Energy Reserve was a regulated strategic energy district, not an isolated fantasy reactor. Its core was the state-owned Southreach Strategic Essence Reserve, Refinery, and Gridworks: storage banks, refining plant, grid dispatch, freight, cooling, research, security, shelters, substations, settlements, roads, canals, and coastal intake.

Visual consequence: the Complex must read as state-scale linked infrastructure. Pipes, rails, roads, reserve cells, cooling, power routing, containment, and emergency segmentation must remain functional and traceable even after destruction.

### The Bloomfall

During a strategic reserve-balancing cycle, multiple storage banks entered uncontrolled resonance. Isolation failed, containment rings sheared, and energized Essence entered the atmosphere, cooling systems, groundwater, drains, terrain, and living tissue. Infrastructure spread the contamination south during the first day. Biological adaptation appeared within weeks. Some reactor sectors still restart, stabilize, vent, purge, overflow, and breach roughly two decades later.

Visual consequence: contamination is aerosol, condensate, runoff, saturated vapor, mineral residue, charged rain, contaminated soil, equipment deposits, and biological uptake. It is not a universal strand texture.

### The Shattercore

The north is the source wound: vitrified concrete, oxidized steel, dead service basins, fractured containment crowns, cycling reserve banks, false-safe dormant intervals, energized drainage, machine-organic accretion, evacuation evidence, and extreme saturation.

Visual consequence: this is the worst direct impact zone and must feel catastrophic before it feels supernatural.

### The Mutation Belt

The center is former managed land—farms, woodland, drainage, roads, substations, and settlements—reorganized by finite adaptive mutation families: vascular overgrowth, mineralization, capacitance, sensory adaptation, and machine graft where the host permits it.

Visual consequence: severe mutation must remain host-specific and functional. Trees, reeds, grazers, harts, runoff, soil, and power infrastructure cannot share one decorative effect.

### The Living Marsh

The southern ancient wetland stopped the oceanward front through layered microbial, algal, fungal, peat, root, and animal filtration. Contamination is bound, moved, mineralized, stored, and sometimes sacrificed; it is not erased or converted into free Essence. Coordinated behavior is proven. Consciousness, a voice, an avatar, a hive mind, or a central controller is not.

Visual consequence: the marsh is black, wet, heavy, dangerous, biologically busy, and visibly processing poison. Simultaneous route closure, sacrificial tissue, flow redirection, and low-saturation pockets imply intelligence without depicting a marsh god.

### Creatures and Aberrants

- Blackbloom Harts sense saturation through conductive antler membranes.
- Rootback Grazers carry soil, filtration roots, insects, and seed communities.
- Mirejaws use pressure-sensing filtration anatomy and flow patterns.
- Sump Eels store temporary charge and can bridge live infrastructure.
- Latchhounds are infrastructure-adapted pack hunters, not generic robots.
- Bellwether is an ecological amplifier affecting migration and trait expression, not a giant-deer boss.
- Switchmother is a Monstrosity/Aberrant whose body routes charge through Splicefield, not a flesh blob or Abomination.
- The Last Shift is a human-origin machine-organic collective repeating emergency procedures; personhood remains unresolved.

Visual consequence: stronger horror comes from functional mutation, scars, altered load paths, containment pressure, ecology, and ambiguous personhood. Correct base anatomy remains mandatory.

### Blackbloom versus Seven-Phase Corruption

Blackbloom Exposure is environmental contamination and adaptation. Seven-Phase Corruption is irreversible soul-level Infusion Corruption. They may interact, but they are not the same process and must not share a single red/purple veiny visual language.

## V3 tone lock

Bloomfall V3 is:

- contaminated;
- irradiated by magical Essence;
- tragic;
- mature;
- severely mutated;
- polluted;
- dangerous;
- oppressive;
- physically grounded;
- unmistakably caused by Southreach's industrial release.

Beauty may emerge from atmosphere, scale, survival, and ecological function. Beauty may not sanitize consequence.

## Failed assumptions removed

- Bloomfall as a clean scenic magitech biome.
- Generic near-future concept art with light contamination dressing.
- Universal glowing strands or tendrils.
- One purple/cyan palette across every host and scene.
- Industrial ruins without reserve, grid, cooling, freight, or containment logic.
- Safe, elegant creatures with a glow effect.
- Bellwether as a noble deer or trophy boss.
- Switchmother as either a robot or an arbitrary flesh-and-cable heap.
- Living Marsh as a harmless bioluminescent swamp.
- Marsh coordination represented by a face, brain, god, or central tree.
- Resource art as a pristine fantasy specimen sheet.
- Expedition art as a clean heroic lineup.
- Local Atlas art that is attractive but cannot support the locked POIs and routes.
