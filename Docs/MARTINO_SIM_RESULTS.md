   · The ladder pays: a phase-six build wins meaningfully more than a clean one across the same encounters — canon's claim, now measured. Phase 7 remains the hard end, and nothing above it is playable.
   · Lone-wolf gap on hard ground is 90% — Procurator · Supply Line genuinely needs a party, which is the design.
   · Species spread is 1%; the ceilings tilt play without deciding it.

MODEL ASSUMPTIONS — the part to argue with
   · Attribute growth: primary attribute reaches its species ceiling near level 80,
     secondary near the cap, untouched attributes drift to about 5. Canon fixes the
     rungs and the ceilings, not the curve.
   · Node weights: every one of the ~450 nodes now carries something the popout can
     show; roughly 250 of them carry fight arithmetic, and the rest are world numbers
     — carry weight, lock times, market margins — real in play, outside this model.
   · Enemy statlines are invented to canon's descriptions, not measured from a build.
   · Trades: the column is treated as fighting on ground it prepared, which is what
     makes Architecture measurable. That is a defensive day and not an ambush.
   · Chemistry's corruption-pace cut is real in the world and deliberately unmodelled:
     one engagement has no ladder clock to slow down.
   · One fight at a time, except where a section says otherwise — the trades and the
     day-of-four both run attrition, which is where a trade decides anything.
