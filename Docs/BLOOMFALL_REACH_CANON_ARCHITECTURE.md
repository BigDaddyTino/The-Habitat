## 1. Core proposition

Bloomfall Reach is the Living Ruin: a former strategic energy district built around the peninsula's largest integrated Essence reserve, refinery, and generating complex. When its containment system cascaded, Essence entered the biosphere instead of dissipating. The north remains an industrial wound; the central country is an adaptive ecological pressure cooker; and the southern marsh has become a living sink that consumes the contamination faster than it spreads. The region asks one local question without answering the global campaign: when life adapts to humanity's worst industry, is that recovery, containment, or the birth of something new?

The spatial law is immutable:

`THE SHATTERCORE → THE MUTATION BELT → THE LIVING MARSH`

The Shattercore shows what humanity broke. The Mutation Belt shows what the Blackbloom creates. The Living Marsh shows what happens when nature stops dying and starts adapting.

**Owner decision:** **Southreach Energy Reserve**, usually **Southreach**. “Bloomfall Reach” is the post-disaster name that preserves the old regional shorthand while replacing its promise of energy security with the catastrophe everyone remembers.

## 3. Facility identity

| Candidate | Common name | Fit and operator implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Southreach Strategic Essence Reserve, Refinery, and Gridworks** | **Southreach Complex** or **the Complex** | Complete, bureaucratic, industrial, and ominous only in hindsight. “Strategic” implies state authority; the functional nouns allow contracted operators. |
| **Sablewater Essence Refinery and Grid Works** | **Sablewater Works** | Grounded public-utility language and strong geographic identity. Implies less about storage scale and could sound municipally owned. |

**Canonical facility name:** **Southreach Strategic Essence Reserve, Refinery, and Gridworks**. In dialogue, signage, and UI it is **the Southreach Complex**. The long name appears on plans, legal records, and surviving seals; characters almost never say it aloud.

### Canonical operator architecture: `MULTI_AGENCY_STRATEGIC_FACILITY`

- The state owned the strategic reserve and grid asset.
- **Aegis Extraction Consortium** held the industrial operating contract: intake, refining, storage logistics, and saleable output. This fits its existing position as the peninsula's dominant buyer/refiner without making the entire site a private kingdom.
- **National Defense Directorate** controlled strategic-reserve security, emergency authority, restricted zones, and classified inventories.
- **Meridian Arcane Institute** certified containment models, staffed an on-site research/assay wing, and published the respectable safety case. It did not run the plant.
- A civilian grid administration bought and dispatched power but does not need to become a faction record unless later stories require it.
- **Helix Arcanobiotics** has no confirmed operating role. A hidden procurement, specimen, or experimental-storage connection is permissible evidence for the deferred mystery, never established fact in this phase.
- The **Abomination Containment Authority** was not the plant regulator. It responded to Seven-Phase cases during and after the evacuation; treating it as the Blackbloom authority would collapse two different corruptions.

This division produces credible shared responsibility while leaving sabotage, suppressed tests, and safeguard removal unresolved.

## 4. The Bloomfall chronology

The Bloomfall occurred roughly two decades before the present day. No exact calendar year is established. This is old enough for a mature adaptive ecosystem and recent enough for survivors, records, liabilities, and active machinery.

| Time | Locked architecture |
| --- | --- |
| **T−years** | Southreach accumulates strategic reserves, refining capacity, and grid generation in one cross-coupled complex. Support settlements, freight lines, cooling canals, and a southern intake port grow around it. |
| **T−days** | Official logs show rising load-balancing errors and containment drift. Surviving copies disagree about whether these were routine, falsified, or suppressed. This discrepancy is evidence, not a solved cause. |
| **T+0** | During a reserve-balancing and grid-dispatch cycle, multiple storage banks enter an uncontrolled resonance. The official account calls it a control-system cascade. |
| **T+minutes to hours** | Automatic isolation fails across cross-coupled banks. Containment rings shear, the primary venting path ruptures, and energized Essence aerosol, condensate, and runoff enter the industrial district. The Shattercore is formed. |
| **T+6–24 hours** | The release follows drainage, cooling channels, prevailing wind, and living tissue south. Emergency systems continue cycling, spreading contamination through infrastructure meant to contain it. |
| **T+1–4 days** | Workers and nearby settlements evacuate through Ashline Exchange and emergency shelters. Some routes close too early; others remain open after they are unsafe. State, Aegis, and local casualty lists never reconcile. |
| **T+1–3 weeks** | Plants stop merely dying and begin adapting. Animals exhibit stable heritable-looking changes within impossible timeframes. The term **Blackbloom** moves from field slang into official quarantine language. |
| **T+3–8 weeks** | The transformation front reaches the ancient southern wetlands. Root, peat, fungal, algal, and microbial layers bind contaminated water and accumulate Essence instead of collapsing. |
| **T+2–6 months** | Survey teams confirm that the oceanward advance has stopped. The marsh grows stranger and more saturated, but the coastline beyond it remains outside the active spread. The Living Marsh becomes containment and hazard at once. |
| **T+years** | Reactor sectors still restart, vent, purge, and fail. The Mutation Belt continues adapting. The marsh's absorption front moves locally with seasons and harvesting pressure but has not resumed its march to the sea. |

### Official account

A cascading industrial control and containment failure during strategic load balancing drove multiple Essence storage banks into uncontrolled resonance. Isolation systems failed faster than the facility could segment the reserve, causing a catastrophic regional release.

This account is credible, technically sufficient, and canon-safe. It does not have to be false merely because it is incomplete.

### Unresolved truth

Contradictory clocks, missing interlock records, impossible inventory totals, unauthorized research traffic, and physical evidence of safeguards being unavailable leave room for sabotage, experimental storage, deliberate safeguard removal, a buried site condition, or political cover-up.

**DEEP-MYSTERY TRUTH: DEFERRED.** Regional quests may establish incompatible evidence. None may identify the true cause, final culprit, global conspiracy, or campaign antagonist.

## 5. Subregion packages

### The Shattercore

**Identity:** the source—one enormous failed industrial system and its support zone, not a random ruin field.

- **Local geography:** northern third of the future child scene; main complex and fractured ring on the high industrial shelf, freight/evacuation corridor west and south, reserve vaults beneath, cooling channels draining toward the Belt. Its world edge remains the locked Magic-Torn Wasteland and Riverlands boundaries.
- **Environment:** vitrified concrete, oxidized steel, crystalline deposition, dead service basins, heatless luminescence, intermittent condensation, and machine-organic accretion around still-powered equipment.
- **Structures:** containment ring segments, reactor/refinery towers, storage vaults, switching yards, rail/freight exchange, emergency shelters, tunnels, control galleries, pumps, and ward emitters belonging to the same campus.
- **Hazards:** high saturation, arc discharge, pressure venting, structural collapse, automated defenses, energized runoff, false-safe dormant intervals, and localized gravity distortion caused by equipment—not the regional physics failure that defines the Magic-Torn Wasteland.
- **Ecology:** sparse but concentrated; scavengers nest in warm conduits, engineered maintenance organisms fuse to machinery, and Blackbloom growth follows cable runs and coolant channels.
- **Traversal:** elevated service decks, maintenance tunnels, power-gated doors, damaged rail beds, and timed crossings keyed to reactor cycles. Safe movement is technical knowledge, not wilderness navigation.
- **Human presence:** NDD perimeter teams, Aegis claim crews, Meridian observers, licensed salvage parties, Wardens, and unauthorized scavengers. No stable civilian settlement inside the core.
- **Event behavior:** sector restart, purge, venting, bank overflow, defense wake, and temporary dormant windows.
- **Resource profile:** existing Essence in dangerous contaminated stores; Reserve Glass; Gridcore Alloy; rare intact controls. Stormglass is not assumed to occur here naturally.
- **Primary systems:** Essence Saturation, Reactor Cycles, Spell Overcharge, Persistent Damage, Lasting Wounds.
- **Major POIs:** Southreach Complex, the Crown Break, Reserve Vault Twelve, Ashline Exchange, Redline Shelter Six.

### The Mutation Belt

**Identity:** the consequence—an adaptive ecological nightmare where Blackbloom changes living systems instead of merely poisoning them.

- **Local geography:** central third; broken farms, managed woodland, drainage basins, substations, service roads, and settlements overtaken by a moving mosaic of habitats.
- **Terrain:** glassroot thickets, soft ground knitted by vascular mats, mineralized tree lines, fast-regrowing clearings, and former fields reorganized around saturation gradients.
- **Plant behavior:** growth seeks energized runoff and warm infrastructure; some plants discharge accumulated Essence, some armor themselves, and some alter routes between visits.
- **Animal behavior:** feeding and migration track saturation rather than season alone; survivors express a limited family of repeatable traits rather than infinite random mutations.
- **Mutation intensity:** usually Active or Surge, with localized Bloom Events. Rare named Aberrants escalate from established bases.
- **Settlements/camps:** only temporary hardened camps and survey platforms. Cairnwood is the operating foothold, not a town.
- **Traversal:** route knowledge expires; vegetation closes roads, herd corridors become temporary paths, and saturation forecasts matter. Movement is ecological reading rather than reactor timing.
- **Weather:** ordinary regional weather remains legible, but contaminated fog, charged rain, and sporefall modify it. This extends Weather; it does not create a second climate system.
- **Resource extraction:** high yield and repeatable risk. Harvesting sink organisms can open routes while raising local saturation and future danger.
- **Event behavior:** herd shift, rapid bloom, predator adaptation, sink collapse, sporefall, and Aberrant escalation.
- **Resource profile:** Sinkroot Fiber, Capacitor Tissue, Blackweir Resin traded north from the marsh, and recoverable mundane salvage.
- **Primary systems:** Adaptive Mutation, Harvesting Consequences, Nature, Gathering & Harvest, Professions, the Living World.
- **Major POIs:** Glassroot Observatory, the Walking Orchard, Splicefield Substation, Cairnwood Camp, the Long Graze.

### The Living Marsh

**Identity:** the answer nature produced—the ecosystem that learned to consume the catastrophe, without proof that it intended to.

- **Local geography:** southern third to the protected coastline; distributary water, peat shelves, root islands, flooded industrial remnants, tidal channels, and an absorption front between Blackweir and Heartfen.
- **Water system:** contaminated runoff enters through northern channels; sediment, root baffles, fungal mats, algae, and microbial films strip and bind active Essence as water moves south. The open ocean remains beyond the arrested front.
- **Biological mechanism:** layered sequestration. Microbial mats change the chemical/magical availability of contamination; mycelium distributes it; peat stores it; roots and sink organisms mineralize it into tissue and resin. No layer solves the problem alone.
- **Flora:** sinkroot buttresses, charge-bladder reeds, blackweir resin growths, predatory raft-vines, and quietwater microbial films.
- **Fauna:** aquatic filter feeders, ambush predators, wading grazers, and animals whose territories maintain or damage absorption structures.
- **Routes:** channels move with root growth, tides, and harvesting. Clan route knowledge can help, but no faction owns the marsh and no map stays permanently correct.
- **Intelligence clues:** repeated route openings, coordinated root closures, sacrifice of saturated tissue, and responses to harvesting can be read as distributed intelligence or as complex ecology. No voice, avatar, or hive-mind is canonized.
- **Human presence:** small seasonal research/salvage platforms, guide camps at the margin, and no permanent sovereign settlement in the deep marsh.
- **Safe zones:** Lantern Pools are locally low-saturation because surrounding organisms pull contamination outward. “Safe” means measurable and temporary, not harmless.
- **Resource profile:** Sinkroot Fiber, Blackweir Resin, Quietwater Culture, and high-risk contaminated tissue. Taking them weakens containment.
- **Event behavior:** absorption pulse, root migration, black tide, sink failure, nocturnal bloom, and megafauna passage.
- **Primary systems:** Marsh Absorption, Harvesting Consequences, Essence Saturation, Environment, the Living World.
- **Major POIs:** the Blackweir, Drowned Intake, Lantern Pools, the Reedless Mile, Heartfen.

## 7. Taxonomy architecture

- **Seven-Phase Corruption** is character/soul corruption from infusion. It advances a person's irreversible phase floor toward Abomination. Player-facing short label: **Infusion Corruption** when contrast is necessary.
- **Blackbloom Exposure** is environmental contamination and adaptation caused by radiated Essence in Bloomfall Reach. It uses exposure/saturation language, never phase numbers. Player-facing short label: **Blackbloom Exposure**.
- The phenomena may interact: exposure can destabilize a dose, an infused person can react strongly to saturation, and Blackbloom may exploit tissue already altered by infusion. Neither automatically advances or substitutes for the other unless a specific future rule says so.
- **Aberrant** is an orthogonal regional designation/tag: an organism or designed construct with an extreme, stable Blackbloom mutation profile. It is not a race, parent taxonomy, synonym, or replacement for its base class.
- **Abomination** remains a former person at completed Seven-Phase Corruption. A Blackbloom-mutated human is not an Abomination without that infusion history. An Abomination may additionally carry the Aberrant designation.
- **Monstrosity** remains something deliberately made through breeding/engineering. A deliberately engineered Latchhound can be a Monstrosity and later a Blackbloom Aberrant. An animal accidentally mutated by the disaster is not thereby a Monstrosity.
- **Races/peoples** retain their ancestry. Exposure never creates a new race. Conscious exposed inhabitants remain people, with condition and provenance recorded separately.
- **Flora/fungi/microbial mats** belong to regional ecology, not creature/race taxonomy unless the repository later gains a dedicated flora kind.
- **Machine-organic hybrids** retain their designed base where one exists. Accidental hybrids without a valid base classification must be flagged for taxonomy review rather than forced into Monstrosity.

Repository-compatible recommendation for Prompt 3: store `blackbloomExposure`/`bloomfallDesignation` as bounded metadata on approved creature/character proposals, not as a new StoryEntry kind or race. Exact schema work requires owner approval and must remain additive.
