# Bloomfall Reach Visual Bible

Status: **DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE - INACTIVE**  
Date: 2026-08-25  
Canon basis: Bloomfall Reach Prompt 3  
Visual package: Bloomfall Reach Prompt 4

This document is the consistency authority for Bloomfall Reach candidate art. It does not activate artwork, define Atlas geometry, establish character faces, or resolve deferred canon.

## Regional read

Every wide Bloomfall composition must read north to south as one causal system:

`Southreach industrial containment failure -> Shattercore release -> Mutation Belt adaptation -> Living Marsh absorption -> protected coast`

The transition follows drainage, damaged infrastructure, and ecology rather than three decorative horizontal stripes. Bloomfall is an industrial and ecological disaster inside the existing Martino world, not a separate fantasy biome.

## Shared Martino language

- **Rendering:** AAA cinematic realism, grounded near-future magitech, mature industrial/ecological horror, believable human scale, restrained depth of field.
- **Materials:** wet charcoal concrete, oxidized structural steel, blackened ceramic containment material, dull brass/copper service fittings, stained glasslike mineral deposition, heavy rubberized expedition textiles.
- **Essence:** contained cyan to storm-blue light, physically motivated by conduits, reserve vessels, instrumentation, vapor, or mineral charge. It is not omnipresent neon.
- **Blackbloom:** bruised green, peat black, smoky teal, bone-grey mineral tissue, and limited cyan internal charge. Avoid the repeated shorthand of black skin plus neon veins.
- **Contrast with Magic-Torn:** Bloomfall obeys gravity and hydrology. Its anomaly is infrastructure-fed biological adaptation, not broken physical law or purple reality distortion.
- **Lighting:** overcast daylight, cold industrial work lights, amber emergency lamps, bioluminescent marsh organisms, and low atmospheric scatter. Light always has a source.
- **Prohibitions:** no wizard towers, steampunk ornament, cyberpunk signage, generic toxic-green wasteland, random tentacles, glossy plastic architecture, modern brands, fake text, baked map labels, icons, routes, borders, or UI.

## Southreach Complex

### Architecture

Southreach was a strategic national Essence reserve, refinery, and gridworks before it was a ruin. Its silhouette uses enormous reserve banks, cylindrical refining towers, nested containment rings, blocky service halls, elevated conduits, switching yards, freight rails, gantries, cooling channels, and hardened emergency structures. Damage reveals the original system instead of erasing it.

### Materials and construction

- Thick poured charcoal concrete with aggregate exposed at breaks.
- Dark oxidized steel frames and service bridges.
- Black ceramic or glass-composite containment liners.
- Dull brass/copper buswork and pipe collars.
- Sparse cyan Essence visible inside engineered vessels and conduits.
- Faded safety color fields may appear, but never legible generated text or real-world branding.

### Containment-ring design

Rings are thick segmented industrial hoops with radial braces, inspection galleries, insulated buswork, and visible service access. They repeat across exterior banks and the core chamber so the facility reads as one campus. Broken rings shear, sag, and expose plausible structure; they do not float without support.

### Grid design and signage

The grid is a functional hierarchy of reserve busways, ceramic insulators, armored cables, switching frames, and substations. Surviving signage is limited to abstract hazard bands, numbering blocks, and blank plates reserved for later programmatic treatment. No fake alphabets.

### Living ruin behavior

Active sectors cycle, purge, vent, illuminate, and move obsolete machinery. Machine-organic growth follows warm conduits, condensate, cable trenches, and energy leaks. It does not decorate every surface uniformly.

## Subregions

### The Shattercore

Cold high industrial shelf; shattered reserve structures; service rail and freight scale; vitrified concrete; exposed containment systems; sparse concentrated growth; controlled cyan venting; small people or vehicles used sparingly for scale. The facility remains the visual cause of the region.

### The Mutation Belt

Former farms, managed woods, drainage works, roads, pylons, and substations remain recognizable under adaptation. Vegetation reorganizes along saturation gradients; mineral roots bridge ditches; animals display habitat-specific traits; bruised greens and glass-grey structures dominate. Weather is still weather, modified by charge and sporefall rather than reality distortion.

### The Living Marsh

Low, hydrologically believable wetlands: black reflective water, distributary channels, peat shelves, root islands, flooded industrial remnants, fungal filters, and quiet luminous pools. Organisms visibly capture, route, store, or precipitate Essence. The marsh is beautiful because it performs a coherent function, not because it is ornamental.

## Blackbloom morphology

- **Vascular behavior:** energized pathways follow sap, nerves, roots, mycelia, water, and conductive machine interfaces.
- **Mineral behavior:** glassroot and Reserve-derived deposition forms ribs, antler channels, plates, lenses, or filtration lattices appropriate to the host.
- **Adaptive behavior:** traits solve local problems such as charge storage, herd coordination, filtration, camouflage, or grid access.
- **Surface range:** peat-black wet membranes, bark, fur, mineral translucency, resin, scarred flesh, oxide, and fungal bloom. Do not standardize every host.
- **Motion implication:** growth leans toward flow and charge; creatures alter nearby herd or plant behavior; infrastructure becomes an energy habitat.
- **Taxonomy boundary:** Blackbloom is environmental Essence exposure and adaptation. It is not Seven-Phase soul corruption.

## Living Marsh systems

- **Roots:** buttressed, braided, hollow-filtered, and channel-spanning; they redirect water without forming impossible waterfalls.
- **Fungi:** lantern colonies use clustered translucent caps, hanging spore vessels, and restrained blue-green/amber emission.
- **Water treatment:** dark contaminated inflow separates through root beds, microbial films, resin weirs, and mineral precipitation into calmer luminous pools.
- **Filtration organisms:** colonial rather than monumental; repeated functional forms suggest coordination without depicting a central intelligence.
- **Coast:** shallow channels and Drowned Intake remnants support later marine access; the established world coastline remains unchanged.

## Expedition equipment

- Layered dark field suits, sealed hoods or masks, replaceable filters, matte armor plates, rubberized boots, and contamination gaiters.
- Instruments are rugged analog-digital magitech: caged cyan sample lights, brass/steel sensor frames, dosimeter-like gauges, specimen tubes, and cable probes.
- Wardens favor mobile protection and field weapons; Meridian ecologists carry sampling and observation gear; NDD security is heavier and standardized; salvage roles use harnesses, cutting tools, and insulated packs; guides prioritize light footing and route markers.
- Vehicles, when shown, are low, sealed, serviceable utility machines rather than futuristic supercars.

## Aberrant silhouette rules

### The Bellwether

Blackbloom Hart lineage; large but biologically supportable cervid mass; functional mineral antler arrays that sense or redistribute Essence; commanding posture and nearby herd response; frightening awareness without a human face or expression.

### Switchmother

Monstrosity plus Aberrant designation; body structurally integrated with the Splicefield switching yard; cables, busbars, transformer housings, and biological mass perform load-routing and defensive functions. Never a freestanding flesh robot.

### Other named Aberrants

Future images must begin from each established base creature, retain a readable functional silhouette, and document scale against a person or known structure. Named status does not erase taxonomy.

## Character continuity

Prompt 3 contains prose metadata for Keira Ansel, Tomas Vey, Selene Ward, Mara Quill, Jaro Fen, and Nalia Reed, but Prompt 4 selected no definitive named-character portrait. The expedition ensemble is a role and equipment study using provisional, non-identifying figures. No face, age detail, or costume in that image becomes canonical for a named character. Mender remains available for later machine-organic development under its existing canon.

## Mystery protection

Images may show contradictory clocks, absent safeguards, unexplained growth response, coordinated filtration, and machinery that appears to remember procedures. They must not identify the true cause, confirm Helix involvement, name a culprit, prove marsh sentience, prove Last Shift consciousness, or establish main-campaign relevance. The intended reaction is suspicion, never solution.

## Continuity checklist

Before approving future Bloomfall art, confirm:

1. Essence light is physically sourced and matches Martino's cyan/storm-blue language.
2. Blackbloom adaptation has a host- and habitat-specific function.
3. Southreach construction uses the same ring, reserve, grid, and material vocabulary.
4. Hydrology, gravity, architecture, anatomy, and human scale remain plausible.
5. The depicted subregion is immediately distinguishable.
6. No generated text, UI, branding, mystery answer, faction invention, or campaign connection appears.
7. Named-character appearance remains consistent with approved metadata or explicitly provisional.
