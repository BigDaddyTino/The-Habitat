# Bloomfall Reach Visual Bible V2

Status: owner-review candidate only. Production art activation remains paused.

This document supersedes the V1 visual treatment for future Bloomfall artwork. It does not supersede story canon, Atlas topology, POI coordinates, routes, or runtime configuration.

## Canonical visual identity

Bloomfall Reach, "The Living Ruin," is the southeast region transformed after the Bloomfall at the Southreach Energy Reserve roughly two decades ago. Its public-facing cause remains an industrial containment failure; its deeper cause remains unresolved.

The regional sequence is geographic and causal:

- north: The Shattercore and the broken Southreach Strategic Essence Reserve, Refinery, and Gridworks;
- center: The Mutation Belt, where roads, fields, drains, woodland, pylons, and settlements remain legible beneath host-specific adaptation;
- south: The Living Marsh, where flat distributary hydrology and biological filtration absorb and store contamination before the coast.

The visual tone is grounded AAA realism: rugged near-future magitech, mature industrial and ecological horror, practical human scale, and beauty that follows from material and biological function.

## Material and light law

- Primary built materials: charcoal concrete, oxidized steel, black containment ceramic, dull brass and copper, wet glass, real cable insulation, weathered road aggregate, and practical textiles.
- Primary natural materials: peat, silt, reeds, bark, leaf tissue, fungal membranes, mineralized bone, black water, algae, mud, and salt-weathered coast.
- Essence light is sparse and source-bound. Cyan or storm blue may appear inside vessels, conduits, instrument displays, mineral charge structures, adapted roots, fungi, or organs.
- Every light needs a source and must affect nearby material plausibly. Darkness retains surface detail; glow never substitutes for form.
- Violet belongs only to controlled Magic-Torn Atlas cues. It is not Bloomfall's universal visual language.

## Physical foundation

Begin every image with a world that works before adding transformation.

- Roads connect destinations and respect grade.
- Rivers and drains flow downhill and join believable watersheds.
- Marsh water remains flat unless a real drop in elevation is visible.
- Structures show supports, joints, access, load paths, pipe termination, electrical routing, and human scale.
- Animals begin with correct skeletons, joints, musculature, feet, hide, and weight distribution.
- Plants begin with recognizable roots, stems, bark, vascular tissue, leaves, fungi, or microbial beds.
- Tools meet hands cleanly; straps, hoses, packs, and protective equipment have practical attachment logic.

## Blackbloom morphology

Blackbloom is environmental exposure, adaptation, and ecological transformation. It changes a host according to the host's material and the function being selected.

- Plant: altered vascular structures, mineralized bark, functional roots, leaf changes, or unusual but load-bearing growth geometry.
- Animal: supportable anatomy, hardened mineral tissue, altered sensory structures, charge storage, or a locomotion-relevant adaptation.
- Fungus: filtration membrane, sparse source-based luminescence, or dense mycelial processing beds.
- Water: suspended particulate, chemical separation, precipitation pools, or visible flow redirection.
- Industrial structure: discoloration, crystallization, biological intrusion, insulation failure, or localized electrical interference.

The same strand, pod, vein, or glossy surface treatment must never be stamped across unrelated hosts.

## Blackbloom versus Seven-Phase Corruption

These are visually and canonically distinct.

- Blackbloom is host-specific environmental adaptation and ecological transformation.
- Seven-Phase Corruption is soul-level Infusion Corruption.

Do not collapse them into a shared red, purple, veiny, reality-fracture, or luminous-filament language. Bloomfall imagery must not answer unresolved Seven-Phase or campaign mysteries.

## Atlas raster law

The world and local Atlas rasters are unlabeled environmental base art. Authoritative boundaries, hits, selection, labels, POIs, and routes remain vector/data layers.

- No baked region boundary, polygon edge, route, POI, label, icon, legend, compass, or grid.
- Terrain transitions carry geographic meaning through relief, hydrology, canopy, geology, settlement, and land use.
- World geography is constrained by the locked world topology and fixed Port Arcadia/Ignit Island anchors.
- The local Atlas is constrained by three subregion polygons, 15 fixed POIs, and two fixed routes.
- Alignment overlays are review evidence only and never enter the selected raster.
- Detail hierarchy is sparse and readable: major rivers, roads, ridges, settlements, fields, forests, facilities, and wetlands must remain identifiable at native pixels.

## Asset-specific anchors

- World Atlas: preserve the Desert, Grand Rift and nested Death Canyon, Red Forest, High Cliffs, Grand Lake, Riverlands, Magic-Torn Wasteland, Bloomfall Reach, Peninsula, Port Arcadia, and Ignit Island.
- Bloomfall hero: marsh foreground, altered countryside midground, and Southreach/Shattercore background should read through geography.
- Shattercore: broken containment and industrial catastrophe with rail, road, structure, and human-scale logic.
- Southreach exterior: distinguish intake, process blocks, reserve vessels, cooling, switchyard, freight, and service access.
- Southreach interior: supported galleries, access, pipes, vessel, bulkheads, and a localized breach.
- Mutation Belt: keep former managed land recognizable beneath localized tree, root, reed, and animal adaptation.
- Living Marsh day: real water, peat, reeds, fungi, roots, remnant infrastructure, and transport scale.
- Living Marsh night: sparse source-based fungal/biological light; no universal glow.
- Bellwether: one credible cervid body, four weight-bearing limbs, cloven hooves, skull-mounted antlers, restrained functional mineral adaptation, and coherent herd response.
- Switchmother: one recognizable biological Monstrosity with anchoring, locomotion, charge storage, routed interfaces, and real switchgear integration; secondary organisms must be described as attached only when an attachment is visibly proven.
- Marsh coordination: show simultaneous physical responses in separate beds and channels; do not show a brain, face, god, telepathy effect, connecting light, or confirmed central intelligence.
- Flora/resources: six separated, materially distinct resource specimens; neutral blank scale/label fixtures may be present but do not count as specimens and contain no generated labeling.
- Bloomstorm: wind, rain, water, vegetation, infrastructure, vehicle, and ordinary wildlife react differently according to physics.
- Expedition: exactly five generic masked roles with correct anatomy, grips, tools, protective equipment, and distinct silhouettes; no canonical named faces.
- Local Atlas: strict top-down 3:2 terrain with Shattercore north, Mutation Belt center, Living Marsh south, and visual support beneath every fixed overlay position.

## PROHIBITED VISUAL DRIFT

- No universal magic strands.
- No baked Atlas boundaries.
- No texture smearing.
- No fake microdetail.
- No meaningless industrial geometry.
- No neon-everywhere corruption.
- No pseudo-text.
- No generic AI fantasy composition.

Also prohibited: decorative spaghetti roots or cables, repeated cyan pods, floating rings, glossy material collapse, all-over contour meshes, random anatomy, duplicated limbs, fused tools, unsupported architecture, arbitrary path networks, purple Bloomfall language, UI, brands, watermarks, and mystery answers.

## Clean-reference policy

- V1 Bloomfall rasters are historical `V1_SUPERSEDED_CANDIDATE` evidence, not style references.
- Legacy world rasters may be inspected for forensic comparison but are excluded from V2 style generation.
- Map generation uses clean geometry/anchor guides plus clean terrain references.
- Scene generation uses written canon and clean Martino material, terrain, coast, industrial, or logistics references selected for the subject.
- Every asset is generated fresh. An iteration that fails anatomy, coherence, topology, or artifact QA remains preserved with `V2_REVISE` or `V2_REJECTED` status.

## Resolution and derivation policy

Report native resolution exactly. Do not describe a simple enlargement as a high-resolution master.

- V2 scene candidates retain the built-in generator's native 1672 x 941 output.
- V2 Atlas candidates retain the highest available native 1536 x 1024 output.
- No V2 selected image is a simple upscale.
- The inactive world and local runtime candidates are byte-identical native-resolution copies, not separately detailed masters.

## QA gate

Every selected asset is scored from 0 to 10 for:

1. AAA realism
2. material fidelity
3. fine detail
4. physical coherence
5. Martino identity
6. canon accuracy
7. artifact cleanliness
8. composition
9. lighting
10. visual storytelling

The hard acceptance floor is 8.0 in every category. Flagship 9+ is a target, not a claim to be manufactured. Review happens at native resolution and includes 100%-pixel crops for architecture, vegetation, water, creature anatomy, materials, industrial detail, and human/equipment contacts.
